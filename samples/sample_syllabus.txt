Course Description
This course explores the interconnected histories of Europe, Africa, and the
Americas from the age of exploration through the age of revolutions. We will
examine commerce, migration, slavery, and cultural exchange across the
Atlantic basin, drawing on primary sources and recent scholarship. Lectures
introduce major themes; discussion sections develop skills in close reading
and historical argumentation.

Learning Objectives
Students who complete this course will be able to analyze primary sources in
historical context, evaluate competing scholarly interpretations, construct
evidence-based arguments, and communicate clearly in written and oral form.
